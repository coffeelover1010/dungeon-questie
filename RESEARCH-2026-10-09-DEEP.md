## Changes and evidence

- **Dalaran Horde key route:** Prison Break In (544) -> Dalaran Patrols (545), plus Key to the City (93680), then Blood in the Streets (92434) -> Heart of Disruption (96984). Wordeen Voidglare gives the preparation quests; Modera gives Heart of Disruption, which returns to Voidglare. The key is provided when accepting Heart, not a completion reward. One keyholder can open the gate. The [current dungeon guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/city-of-dalaran-dungeon-overview-location-rewards), [Blood in the Streets](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=92434) and [Heart of Disruption](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96984) support the route. The individual 544, 545 and 93680 pages provide preparation objectives. No unverified minimum levels or Keeper Bel'varil quests were added to this route.
- **Dalaran pickups:** Opportunity for Education is picked up from Rexxie inside the entrance. A Green Sample points to Shylamiir in The Park; Source of Power points to Doctor Martin Felben in the Apothecarium. The same guide supports these directions. Alliance An Alarming Request remains an optional/unverified lead-in rather than a mandatory gate to Heart of Disruption. Starving Arcane's faction remains unresolved.
- **Shaman Elemental Aid:** both faction variants now carry the Shaman restriction instead of unresolved eligibility. [Current weapon-chain reporting](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/unlock-powerful-weapons-with-level-30-shaman-quest-in-wow-forever-383290), including its linked [first-hand report](https://www.reddit.com/r/classicwow/comments/1wvrqal/shaman_ww_weapon_is_in_the_game/), supports the class and Air Totem context. The [Alliance quest page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96264) links Call of Air (94789) and Veyric Thunderhame. The earlier Alliance starting NPC and exact Horde Call of Air ID remain unverified. The Horde beta-cache record sends the Tattered Note to Rau Cliffrunner; older Wowhead text describes another step, so that conflict remains explicit.
- **Wetlands:** [Lost in the Thicket Things](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95647) can be checked directly at Caitlin. Seeking Caitlin is an optional introduction, not a forced trip to Ashenvale. [Highland Hides](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98815) has conflicting reports: the older [Wetlands guide](https://www.wowhead.com/news/every-quest-in-excavation-site-wetlands-wow-forever-383239) names Daily Delivery; a newer player report names Young Crocolisk Skins and says Daily Delivery is unnecessary. Neither is enforced pending verification. [Changing Tastes](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95697) now identifies Borstan in The Drag. [Dragonmaw Rumors](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95663) identifies Zaruk in Hammerfall; [Open the Maw](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95682) describes the uphill approach to the Deathstalker Agent. Reported coordinates remain written directions, not new pickup pins.

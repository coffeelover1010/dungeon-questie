## Sources and what they established

- [Blizzard's October 1 development update, post 4](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-updated-october-1/2360696/4): the beta level cap is now 30; Excavation Site: Wetlands opened, with a stated 26–31 range; Razorfen Downs and Uldaman access expanded. The earlier post in the same thread is September 24, so reading only the first post would miss the update. The addon still reads recruiting level suggestions from the client rather than replacing them with an entrance level or this announcement range.
- [Fresh quest index](https://wowforevertalents.com/quests/): 108 published quest records across all instance types. The collector found 99 dungeon records across 13 groups. All 99 individual quest pages were fetched to a new snapshot; 94 were accepted and five Dalaran records deferred. This is a firsthand beta-cache reporting site, not our own gameplay verification. Individual `sourceDetails` retain underlying cache/log dates where the page exposes them.
- [Wowhead dungeon quest guide, updated October 2](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/dungeons/every-dungeon-quest-location): Wetlands now has named quest entries and pickup leads. Individual quest pages were checked as well. New guide coordinates were not installed as pins because this run did not independently verify their map positions.
- [Searching for the Lost Satchel](https://wowforevertalents.com/quests/searching-for-the-lost-satchel-5722/): a dated beta-log observation identifies Rahauro at Thunder Bluff, Elder Rise, 70.8/31.6. Replaced the ambiguous inside-dungeon pickup direction and added the corresponding outdoor pickup pin. Maur remains the objective/turn-in reference; he is not the starting NPC.
- [A Frightened Request](https://wowforevertalents.com/quests/a-frightened-request-92401/): the dated beta-log observation confirms the existing Sepulcher pickup/pin. Its source was updated; the conflicting Undercity guide coordinate was not adopted.
- [Developer clarification reported by Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/this-is-the-only-new-dungeon-you-can-run-in-this-weeks-wow-forever-build/): Dalaran was not available in this build. The linked original developer post was inaccessible (HTTP 403) during this check, so this availability claim remains an attributed secondary report. Dalaran records were retained for later review, not added as actionable preparation targets.

Searches for the remaining announced dungeons did not establish reliable new quest IDs and routes to import. This is not proof that no quests exist. Preview pages, old client snapshots, SEO guides and same-named quests from other WoW editions are insufficient.

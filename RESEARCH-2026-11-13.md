# Forever dungeon review — 13 November 2026

## Result and scope

Version 0.2.1 preserves the existing provider, tracking, UI and saved-variable
architecture. There are **320 catalogue records**, up from 266: **54 additions**,
no deleted existing quests, and all **29 dungeon groups** have recorded quests.
Numbers below are records before faction filtering, not independent chains or
quests available to one character. Populated is not the same as complete.

| Dungeon | Recorded quests/stages after review |
| --- | ---: |
| The Drowned City | 10 (eight dungeon quests, two Alliance reward handoffs) |
| Krol'dok Stronghold | 15 |
| Alcaz Prison | 15 |
| Blackmaw Hold | 11 |
| The Shaper's Terrace | 6 |

Hall of Thanes, Excavation Site: Wetlands, Ruins of Lordaeron and City of Dalaran
were also checked against current category lists and the current Wowhead guide.
Existing Hall/Wetlands mappings were retained. Unending Torment's five stages are
now explicitly linked in the walkthrough, with its Undercity work kept outside
the dungeon. Wrath of Rath'mael requires 99156 (preceded by 356); The New Plague
requires 451 (preceded by 450 and 447). Their previous root-level readiness was
unsafe on current live data.

- [QuestieDB 1.1.0](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/releases/tag/v1.1.0), released November 12, is installed and was the latest GitHub release when checked. Its actual public contract 4 API was exercised offline. No provider database values or source were bundled into the review inputs.
- The freshly fetched wowforevertalents.com index still had only three Drowned City records and no records for the other four target dungeons. It was not treated as complete.

Research snapshots are in workspace tmp/dungeon-review-20261113, outside the
installed addon. Generator input is data/forever-review-2026-11-13.json. It applies
after previous reviewed overrides, preserving unrelated prior work. Explicit nulls
remove superseded inferred faction masks. Rewards are verified item IDs linked
from quest reward sections; supplied quest items were not mistaken for rewards.

Class restrictions: current provider masks are read at runtime. No added quest
had a positive class mask in the installed provider; two new IDs were absent.
The independent quest pages did not establish a separate exhaustive class audit.
No class restriction was invented. Missing-provider/uncertain records retain
readiness guards. A server offer, accepted quest or actual completion wins over
reference restrictions. This is not live-client validation.

## Important conflicts and exclusions

- Krol'dok guide lists Horde levels of 30 for A Token of Vengeance, Tome of Twilight
  and Eye of Mugg. Individual database records and QuestieDB agree on **38, 39, 40**;
  those values are used. Moulding has separate Alliance/Horde IDs despite neutral
  symbols in the guide. Faction comes from individual quest records.
- Zero minimum levels in provider records are missing information. Verified local
  minima now fill these gaps, including Alcaz treasure and Blackmaw quests.
- Drowned City Alliance quests require the Southshore handoff 95786 after Troll
  Witchery (205). It is explicitly not treated as an optional breadcrumb. The
  guide's abbreviated chain is not used to skip that handoff.
- Somber News (95263) has conflicting/branching source chains from 95262 and 96335.
  Its alternative links are retained with an uncertainty guard, not assumed ready.
- State Secrets (96376/96377/96379), Krol'dok No More! (94969/95056), and Blackmaw
  Pouches variants remain separate IDs with uncertain relationships. They are not
  merged or marked completed merely because another same-name quest was completed.
- **6031 Runecloth and 6032 Sacred Cloth are excluded** from Blackmaw: the individual
  objectives are cloth deliveries to Meilosh in Timbermaw Hold, not dungeon work.
- Dalaran category records 96812 Thicker Than Water, 96609 A Taste of Home and
  96603 Unstable Magic concern outside/city work rather than the dungeon. They were
  not added merely because the database category is City of Dalaran. Record 96602
  More Unstable Magic was found in the category but its individual page could not
  be retrieved; its dungeon association remains unresolved and it was not added.
- Missing pickups, exact chain requirements, variant relationships and reward
  omissions are not filled with guessed NPCs, coordinates, quest IDs or items.
  "No item rewards listed" is not proof that the server awards no items.

## Runtime fixes

Known faction, race and class restrictions still filter records. Unknown eligibility
now stays visible and stops guide readiness. Temporary quest/maximum-level gates
remain visible as blocked/preparing, rather than disappearing as another faction.
Verified sequential catalogue stages share one checklist slot through their reward handoff; ambiguous variants stay separate. The resolver checks known prerequisites before the goal. Unknown chains, missing
pickup sources, scan failures and unevaluable requirements cannot produce readiness.
The UI says **Data incomplete** and retains a short explanation instead of hiding
all uncertainty in source files. Existing accepted/completed precedence, skipped
breadcrumbs, saved settings, Classic APIs and manual interaction behavior remain.

## In-game verification

1. Enable QuestieDB and Dungeon Questie, then `/reload` and `/dungeons`.
2. On Alliance and Horde characters, check all five named dungeons. Low-level
   quests remain visible as blocked; faction-only quests appear on the right side.
3. For Drowned City, leave 95770 or 95786 unfinished: the guide must lead to that
   prerequisite. Complete objectives without turning it in: it must still require
   the turn-in. Hand it in and verify advancement. Brinescale also requires 95773.
4. Check the two Ruins of Lordaeron chains and the Unending Torment continuation.
5. Accept, abandon, finish objectives and turn in a quest. Acceptance should count
   as collected; abandonment must not count as completed; only the real turn-in or
   completion history should mark completion. Skipped breadcrumbs stay distinct.
6. Verify Data incomplete on an unresolved State Secrets/Krol'dok No More record;
   a real offer or acceptance may establish availability without inventing history.
7. Check a Classic dungeon/class quest, saved position/settings, reward tooltips,
   map buttons and quest-log targeting. No unverified interior coordinates were added.

## Offline verification

Passed Lua 5.1 syntax/manifest checks and the provider, walkthrough, action,
chain-grouping, chain-progress, readiness, guide-availability, location, wording,
quest-card, shard-variant, quest-group, group-finder and recruitment checks.

Alliance/Horde across nine classes, prerequisite turn-ins, AND gates, unknown-data
and temporary-condition visibility, accepted/completed states, sequential handoffs,
and nonzero preparation totals on low-level characters.

The installed QuestieDB API finds 316/320 catalogue IDs; absent IDs are the two
older records 3366/3911 and new records 95056/92070. Zero/default provider fields
remain incomplete even when an entity exists. The map audit finds 309 usable
locations and 11 explicit gaps. No old quest was deleted; six pre-existing records
changed (97288, 92422, 95216, 95775, 95776, 96774). The guide-availability suite
exercises 17,280 quest/profile combinations; the provider suite exercises 1,280
acceptance/completion cases. These are offline mocks, not live-client proof.

The per-quest unresolved list contains 32 goal/support records with incomplete
routes, pickups or branch relationships. All remain guarded; this review does
not claim complete server knowledge.
